Sports medicine in Madrid

Sports medicine diagnoses and treats injuries of the musculoskeletal system and guides a safe return to activity. Dr Julio De la Morena is a specialist in Sport and Exercise Medicine. He spent fourteen years with the Real Madrid CF medical services, four of them as first-team doctor, is Chief Medical Officer of the Spanish Rugby Federation and continues to look after professional rugby, padel and football players. He applies the same method to anyone, from weekend runners to people who simply want to walk without pain, at Hospital Memorial Publio Cordón (Pozuelo de Alarcón, Madrid). Consultations are available in English.

What is treated

  • Muscle, tendon and ligament injuries.
  • Knee, ankle, shoulder and spine injuries in athletes.
  • Overload and recurrent injuries.
  • Planning the return to sport after an injury or surgery.

The method

Accurate diagnosis with examination and ultrasound in the same appointment, a plan with timelines and objective criteria, and coordination with physiotherapists, rehabilitation specialists and, when needed, surgeons. The aim is to return as soon as possible, but without re-injury.

Frequently asked questions

Do you see recreational athletes or only professionals?

Everyone. The clinic sees professional rugby, padel and football players, as well as recreational athletes and people who do no sport at all.

Where does Dr Julio De la Morena see patients?

At the Regenerative Therapies and Sports Medicine Unit of Hospital Memorial Publio Cordón, Paseo de la Casa de Campo, 5, 28223 Pozuelo de Alarcón (Madrid). Appointments: +34 913 548 990.

Can I be seen in English?

Yes. Dr De la Morena sees patients in English as well as Spanish.

Would you like your case assessed?

Examination and ultrasound in the same appointment: you leave with a diagnosis and a plan. First appointment with no commitment to treatment: if nothing is indicated, we tell you.
