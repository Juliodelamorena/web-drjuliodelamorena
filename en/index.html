One-stop consultation

Diagnosis and treatment in a single visit

No waiting between tests. In the same appointment you are examined, studied with high-resolution ultrasound and diagnosed and, when appropriate, treatment begins, in coordination with interventional radiology.

  1. I

    Clinical assessment

    History, physical examination and review of any tests you bring.

  2. II

    Ultrasound in clinic

    Dynamic study of tendons, muscles and joints on the spot.

  3. III

    Diagnosis and plan

    An individual treatment plan, clearly explained.

  4. IV

    Treatment and follow-up

    Image-guided procedures when appropriate and scheduled reviews.

Treatments

Regenerative medicine and image-guided procedures

Every treatment is chosen after diagnosis and tailored to the patient. Surgery is reserved for the cases that genuinely need it.

Autologous growth factors

Platelet-rich plasma obtained from your own blood and applied to the injured area, in selected cases of osteoarthritis and tendinopathy.

Ultrasound-guided injections

Treatment placed precisely, with the needle and the target visible on screen.

Musculoskeletal ultrasound

Diagnosis in clinic and monitoring of progress without waiting.

Therapeutic exercise

An individual strength and rehabilitation programme, coordinated with physiotherapy.

Sports medicine

Assessment, injury prevention and return to activity.

Second opinion

Review of diagnoses and surgical recommendations before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Before your first visit

Do I need to be an athlete to come to the clinic?

No. The clinic sees professional rugby, padel and football players, and also people who do no sport and have osteoarthritis, tendinopathies, back pain, sciatica or sprains.

Can knee osteoarthritis be treated without surgery?

In many cases, yes, at least for a time. There are options between conservative treatment and a knee replacement: therapeutic exercise, weight management, ultrasound-guided injections and autologous growth factors when appropriate. Each case is assessed in clinic.

What are autologous growth factors?

A treatment obtained from the patient's own blood, also known as platelet-rich plasma, and applied to the injured area, usually under ultrasound guidance. It is used in selected cases of osteoarthritis and tendinopathy; it is not suitable for every patient.

What is an ultrasound-guided injection?

An injection in which the doctor sees the needle and the structure being treated on the ultrasound screen, so the treatment is placed exactly in the affected tendon or joint.

Can I be seen in English?

Yes. Dr De la Morena sees patients in English and Spanish.

Do you accept health insurance?

No. Consultations are private and are not billed through insurers. You are told the fee when you book, before the visit.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly.
