The doctor

Dr Julio De la Morena Garzón

Dr Julio De la Morena Garzón is a specialist in Sport and Exercise Medicine (Madrid Medical Council no. 281903698). He sees patients at the Regenerative Therapies and Sports Medicine Unit of Hospital Memorial Publio Cordón in Pozuelo de Alarcón (Madrid). He spent fourteen years with the Real Madrid CF medical services, four of them as first-team doctor, and is Chief Medical Officer of the Spanish Rugby Federation. He treats osteoarthritis, tendinopathies, back pain and musculoskeletal injuries in anyone, whether or not they play sport, with ultrasound in the same appointment and treatment without surgery whenever possible. Consultations are available in English.

How he works

One-stop consultation: in a single visit the patient is examined, studied with ultrasound, diagnosed and, when appropriate, treatment begins, in coordination with interventional radiology, physiotherapy and orthopaedic surgery. Surgery is reserved for the cases that genuinely need it. Patients from abroad can have their scans reviewed before travelling.

Training

  • Specialist in Sport and Exercise Medicine (Spanish MIR residency) · Complutense University of Madrid (2006).
  • Degree in Medicine and Surgery · University of Alcalá (1998), top of his year.
  • Good Clinical Practice certification for clinical trials (ICH-GCP E6 R3) · NIDA Clinical Trials Network, National Institutes of Health (2026–2029).
  • Accredited doping-control physician, Spanish Sports Council.

Career

  • 2026 – present · Hospital Memorial Publio Cordón (Pozuelo de Alarcón, Madrid). Regenerative Therapies and Sports Medicine Unit: diagnostic and interventional musculoskeletal ultrasound and ultrasound-guided biological therapies. Development of the hospital's advanced-therapies programme, including a clinical trial in muscle injury.
  • 2022 – present · Spanish Rugby Federation. Chief Medical Officer: clinical lead for the national teams; prevention, diagnosis and safe return-to-play protocols.
  • 2021 – 2026 · Hospital Ruber Internacional (Madrid). Sports, Regenerative and Biomechanics Medicine Unit.
  • 2007 – 2021 · Real Madrid CF. Fourteen years with the club's medical services: academy (2007–2013), Real Madrid Castilla (2013–2017) and first-team doctor (2017–2021).
  • 2012 – 2019 · Hospital Sanitas La Moraleja (Madrid). Sports Medicine Unit.

Teaching and languages

  • "Manuel Clavel Nolla" Award (Spanish National Sports Traumatology Meeting, 2007) for an ultrasound study of the quadriceps tendon in elite weightlifters.
  • Associate lecturer on the master's degrees in Sports Training and Nutrition and in Sports Physiotherapy · Universidad Europea de Madrid (2013–2016); contributing lecturer on the master's in Sports Traumatology · Complutense University of Madrid (2011–2012).
  • Languages: Spanish and English (he completed his secondary education in the United Kingdom). Written communication with patients from abroad in their own language.

Frequently asked questions

What does Dr Julio De la Morena treat?

Osteoarthritis, tendinopathies, low back pain and sciatica, shoulder pain, sprains and muscle injuries, in athletes and in people who do no sport. He uses ultrasound in the same appointment, ultrasound-guided injections and autologous growth factors (platelet-rich plasma) when appropriate.

Does Dr De la Morena speak English?

Yes. Consultations are available in English and Spanish.

How do I book an appointment?

By phone on +34 913 548 990, or with the contact form on drjuliodelamorena.com/en/, and we call you back.

Would you like your case assessed?

Examination and ultrasound in the same appointment: you leave with a diagnosis and a plan. First appointment with no commitment to treatment: if nothing is indicated, we tell you.
